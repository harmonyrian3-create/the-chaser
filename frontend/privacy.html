Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

At The Chaser, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you sign up for our service:

  • Your firm name
  • Your email address
  • Your password (encrypted and securely stored)
  • Client names and email addresses (as provided by you)
  • Receipt images and extracted text (for processing and categorization)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain our receipt-chasing service
  • To send automated reminder emails to your clients
  • To improve our AI categorization and OCR accuracy
  • To send you important account notifications

3. Data Security

We use industry-standard encryption (SSL/TLS) for all data transmission. Passwords are hashed using bcrypt and never stored in plain text. Your data is stored securely in the cloud with access controls.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your data is only shared with:

  • Resend – for sending emails to you and your clients
  • Google Gemini – for AI receipt categorization
  • Neon – for database storage

5. Your Rights

  • You can request a copy of all data we hold about you
  • You can request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • You can unsubscribe from marketing emails

6. Cookies

We use JWT tokens stored in localStorage for authentication. We do not use tracking cookies or analytics cookies.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: privacy@thechaser.com
